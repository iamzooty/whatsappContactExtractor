How to save the WhatsApp Web HTML file?
  1. Open web.whatsapp.com in Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. Click on the group chat whose contacts you want to export.
  3. Click the Group Subject / Name at the top bar to open the Group Info drawer and reveal the participant list.
  4. Press Ctrl + S (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + S (Mac) and choose "Webpage, HTML Only" or "Webpage, Complete".
  5. Upload the saved .html file above and click Extract Contacts.
