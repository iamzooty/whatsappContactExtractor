How to Extract Your Contacts

Follow these three quick steps directly from your computer browser.

1

Open WhatsApp Web

Open WhatsApp Web in your browser, open the target group, and click the top group header to view the full participant list. Then save the page (Ctrl + S or Cmd + S) as an HTML file.

2

Upload the HTML File

Head over to our Extract page, drop your saved .html file into the upload box, select your preferred contact name prefix, and click Extract.

3

Download Your CSV

Preview the extracted contacts on screen and download your neatly formatted .csv spreadsheet with a single click. Ready for Google Contacts or Excel!
